SR Fashion product image upload setup

1. Upload the website files to Hostinger public_html.
2. Keep the image-api folder.
3. Keep the private folder and its .htaccess file.
4. Inside private, create image-upload-key.txt.
5. Put your private image upload password inside that file.
6. Do not share that password publicly.
7. Product images will save under images/products/PRODUCT-CODE/.

Old GitHub images are kept as read-only fallback.
New uploads and deletes are permanent on Hostinger/local website storage.
